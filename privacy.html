Privacy policy

Last updated 2 November 2026.

What the app is

Toby Management is a personal desktop application for Windows, used by its developer only. It is not a commercial service and has no server of its own.

What Google data it uses

With your permission the app reads your Google Calendar list, the events of the calendars you select, and calendar details such as event colour labels. Access is read-only. The app never creates, edits or deletes calendar data.

Where data is stored

Everything the app reads is stored only on the computer it runs on, in the app's own user-data folder. Sign-in tokens are encrypted with Windows user protection. No calendar data, tokens or personal information is sent to any server operated by the developer or to any third party.

How data is used

Calendar data is used solely to display your schedule and to calculate coaching earnings and invoices inside the app. It is not used for advertising, analytics or any other purpose, and it is never shared or sold.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Use Disconnect in the app's Settings to delete the stored sign-in tokens, and remove Toby Management from the third-party connections in your Google Account to revoke access at Google. Deleting the app's user-data folder removes all cached data.
